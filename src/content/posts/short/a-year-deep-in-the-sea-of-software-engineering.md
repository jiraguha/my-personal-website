It's been a year since I dove deep into the sea of modern software engineering. Most of my day-to-day work now revolves around context gathering, architecture, and building autonomous DX loop harnesses.

If there's one thing I can say after this year, it's that my sense of "creation" has shifted. I used to treat it as an act of control — deciding, imposing, building from will. Now it feels more like discovery: looking for structure and pattern that was already there, waiting to be found. I don't own it anymore. I just notice it.

> "Ideas are like fish. If you want to catch little fish, you can stay in the shallow water. But if you want to catch the big fish, you've got to go deeper." — David Lynch, *Catching the Big Fish*

That's closer to how it feels now. Less inventing, more fishing.
